# Known Limitations

- Progress is device-local; it does not sync across browsers or devices.
- Clearing site data removes progress.
- The training cannot inspect a learner’s real inbox, browser certificate, DNS data, or destination redirects.
- URL examples teach the registrable-domain concept with controlled example domains; they do not replace a Public Suffix List parser or security tooling.
- Classification exercises deliberately provide limited evidence. In real situations, independent verification and organizational policy remain necessary.
- The project is educational and does not provide certification or guarantee protection from phishing.
- Accessibility has been designed into semantics, focus states, contrast, reduced motion, and status labels, but a formal third-party WCAG audit has not been performed.
