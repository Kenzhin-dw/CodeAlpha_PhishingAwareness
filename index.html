01 · Fundamentals

Phishing is deception with a destination.

Attackers impersonate a trusted person or service to make someone reveal information, send money, open a file, or visit a harmful page.

1Deceptive contactEmail, text, call, QR code, or social message
→
2Emotional pressureUrgency, fear, trust, curiosity, or reward
→
3Unsafe actionClick, reply, sign in, pay, or open a file
→
4ImpactAccount access, fraud, malware, or data loss
Key idea

Phishing succeeds by influencing decisions—not by looking obviously fake. A polished message can still be malicious.

    09 · Prevention

    Build a pause into high-risk moments.

    A practical checklist for links, sign-ins, accounts, and suspicious messages.

    Before clicking
    • Check the full sender address
    • Preview the actual destination
    • Ask whether the message was expected
    • Question urgency and unusual requests
    Before signing in
    • Read the registrable domain
    • Open a known bookmark or official app
    • Avoid sign-in links in unexpected messages
    • Never share passwords or MFA codes
    Protect accounts
    • Use unique passwords and a password manager
    • Turn on MFA; phishing-resistant options are strongest
    • Keep software and browsers updated
    • Review active sessions and recovery details
    If suspicious
    • Stop interacting with the message
    • Verify via a known phone number or app
    • Report through the appropriate channel
    • Delete only after reporting if policy allows
    10 · Incident response

    Act promptly—not panically.

    An accidental click is a reason to respond, not to hide the incident.

    1. StopDo not enter more information, approve prompts, or keep interacting.
    2. ReportTell your organization’s security or support contact what happened and when.
    3. RecoverIf credentials were entered, use the official service to change the password and sign out other sessions.
    4. StrengthenEnable or verify MFA, review recovery details, and follow the organization’s instructions.
    5. CheckIf a file ran or software may have downloaded, disconnect if policy requires and contact support for a security scan.
    Why speed matters

    Early reporting gives defenders more time to protect accounts and other people who received the same message.
